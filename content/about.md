I am Arman Arisman, an architect based in Indonesia. I've spent over 15 years in building and planning consulting, most of it at Penta Architecture, working in architectural computing—AI, BIM, computational design, and the tools in between—on real projects. I hold a doctoral degree from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), where my research grew directly out of that practice.

My writing grows out of my research interests: the gap between what's claimed about technology implementation in design practice and what actually happens on real projects, and how architects think and work differently once these tools enter the process. I use them myself, and I'm still working out where the claims hold up and where they don't.

I'm also looking for ways back into teaching, since a lot of what I've learned the hard way on projects seems useful to students and younger practitioners figuring out their own footing.
