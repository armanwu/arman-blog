Architectural form-finding and tectonic studies synthesized through code in **Julia** and **Python**—investigating how mathematical geometry can be disciplined into constructible structure and lived phenomenological space. Rather than treating computation as an abstract formal exercise, each work examines how algorithmic rules choreograph gravity, material weight, diurnal light, and human movement.
