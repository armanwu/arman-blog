A collection of early-stage architectural form-finding and spatial studies developed across a wide range of computational design tools—from pure mathematical scripting and algorithmic code to parametric modeling and node-based workflows. Here, computation does not replace design intuition; instead, digital rules and systems generate raw geometric provocations—which are then curated, rationalized, and translated by the architect into tectonic structure, materiality, and lived spatial atmosphere.
