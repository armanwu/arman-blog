About Me

I am Arman Arisman (Arman Wu), an architect and technology manager with over 15 years of experience in public planning. My work lies at the intersection of architectural form, digital systems, and design cognition. Specializing in BIM, computational design, and HCI, I focus on optimizing workflows and bridging multidisciplinary teams. Beyond architecture, I am an active contributor to the Fedora Project and a writer for Fedora Magazine.
